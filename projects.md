## During the doctoral period

- Simulation architecture and supporting technologies for urban multimodal transportation systems based on big data, **National Key R&D Program of China**, 2020–2024, participated.
- A theory-and-data fusion driven modeling method for uninterrupted mixed traffic flow under the intelligent connected environment, **NSFC General Program**, 2023–2026, participated.
- Evolution patterns and forecasting methods of regional traffic congestion based on multi-source heterogeneous big data fusion, **NSFC General Program**, 2018–2023, participated.
- Development and demonstration application of a rail transit train wheel-slip risk monitoring/early-warning and operation control system under adverse weather, **Beijing Municipal Science & Technology Plan**, 2025–2027, participated.
- Research on the construction and development of the Beijing comprehensive three-dimensional transportation network, **Capital High-end Think Tank Project**, 2021–2025, participated.
- Shenzhen Rail Transit Network Operation Control Center (NOCC) Phase II: NCC system, **Ministry of Transport S&T Demonstration Project**, 2022–2024, participated.

I have participated in **more than 10 industry/contract (horizontal) projects** in transportation/rail transit natural science areas during my Ph.D., mainly covering passenger flow forecasting systems, passenger flow simulation systems, and intelligent timetable planning systems for cities including **Beijing, Shenzhen, Chengdu, Chongqing, Suzhou, Wuhan, and Xi’an**. During my master’s period, I participated in more than 10 traffic organization plan design projects during construction.

- Development of passenger flow prediction software for Chengdu Metro Passenger Service Management Platform (NCCC) project, Chengdu Metro Operating Co., Ltd., CNY 200,000.
- Suzhou Metro Network Command Center (NCC) passenger flow prediction project, Suzhou Metro, CNY 1,900,000.
- Suzhou Metro Network Command Center (NCC) network- and line-level simulation project, Suzhou Metro, CNY 1,060,000.
- Testing of urban rail transit network passenger flow simulation system, Ministry of Transport Research Institute, CNY 90,000.
- Research on the construction and development of the Beijing comprehensive three-dimensional transportation network, Beijing Jiaotong University Beijing Integrated Transport Center, CNY 50,000.
- Research on technologies for local failures and impact fluctuation evolution in networked operations of urban rail transit, Ministry of Transport Research Institute, CNY 50,000.
- R&D of a panoramic passenger-flow perception, prediction and analysis platform for urban rail transit networks, Beijing Metro Operation Co., Ltd., CNY 3,760,000.
- R&D of an intelligent train operation plan compilation and management platform for networked urban rail transit, Beijing Metro Operation Co., Ltd., CNY 3,350,000.
